Our Leadership

Strong leadership inspires communities to plant, protect, and preserve trees for a greener and healthier future.

Leading the Green Movement

Our leadership team works with communities, schools, youth groups, volunteers, and environmental partners to coordinate tree planting and conservation activities. Through teamwork and responsible leadership, we aim to create sustainable environmental change.

Chairperson

Project Leadership

Provides overall direction, coordinates tree-planting programs, manages activities, and ensures that environmental goals are achieved.

Secretary

Community Mobilization

Works with local communities to encourage participation, organize volunteers, and promote long-term environmental responsibility.

Environmental Coordinator

Conservation & Education

Guides participants on proper tree planting, environmental conservation, tree care, and sustainable land management.

Leadership Responsibilities

🌱

Planting Coordination

Organizing planting activities and ensuring that teams have the resources and guidance required.

👥

Community Mobilization

Bringing together community members, volunteers, schools, and organizations to participate in tree planting.

📚

Environmental Education

Educating participants about the importance of trees, biodiversity, climate, and environmental conservation.

🌳

Tree Care

Supporting teams in watering, protecting, monitoring, and maintaining planted trees.

📊

Monitoring

Tracking planting activities, tree survival, community participation, and conservation progress.

🤝

Partnerships

Building partnerships with schools, communities, organizations, and environmental stakeholders.

Leadership for a Greener Future

"Every tree we plant represents a commitment to our environment. Through responsible leadership, teamwork, and community action, we can create a greener future for generations to come."
